How To Talk To Me
A website that details your plan of action to talk to me, and what steps you should follow.

Do people DM you "Hello" all the time? Do you respond with a "Hello" back? Then do they ask you a question? You answer the question, but you have a gut feeling that they still need help because they still sound confused? This website is my attempt to get ride of all that by teaching people who work with others online what to do and how to do it. It's goal is to save the sender and the recipient time and effort. 

Inspired by nohello.net, dontasktoask.com, and xyproblem.info.
