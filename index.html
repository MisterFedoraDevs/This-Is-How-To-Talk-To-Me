, This Is How To Talk To Me

* - Without wasting my time (and yours)!

So, you want a few examples?

  • "Hello"
  • @Username
  • "Can you look at this?
  • "I need your help"

Instead:

  • "Hello, can you help me debug this JavaScript. I have looked at it twice and couldn't find the issue." [Linked]
  • @Username "I noticed you like designing webpages. What projects have you worked on. I'm looking for some inspiration."
  • "Hey, I a working on a website for a Law Firm and I was wondering if you could give me feedback on the homepage? I would appreciate it if you can look at it before Sunday."
  • "How are you doing? I was wondering if you can give me some resources to learn HTML. I have already watched a few videos, but they did not help." [Linked]

Please stop texting me '"Hello" 'when you need something.

So, you have a question. It could be about a website, debugging a piece of code, or what projects I have created. There's only one problem, you don't know how to ask! That's okay, but please, figure it out before you hit send.

I, like other people, am very busy! When I check my messages on Slack, or my email, I skim through them. That means I check all the important messages first, and go back to check the rest later. You putting "Hello" as your message automatically categorizes it as #NotImportant.

Want me to respond to you fast? Here's how:

  • I employ you to lead your message off with "Hello" or "How are you?"! It just should not be the full message.
  • Tell me what you need, ASAP! The faster I can read it, the faster I can respond to it.
  • If you want my ideas on a specific topic, tell me exactly what you want me to tell you.
  • Give me all the information. If you need help debugging a program, send me what you have tried in addition to the program itself (most people forget that part).
  • Make your message short and easy to understand if you can. This helps me read it faster.
  • If you have a "deadline" for me, include it at the end of the message. Typically, I add these to my calander so I don't forget to get back with you. Just make sure it is reasonable.
